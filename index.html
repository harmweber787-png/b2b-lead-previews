US Healthcare Organizations from the Public NPPES Registry

A ready-to-use data pack (CSV and JSON) and the Python script that builds it, so you do not have to look up and clean the records yourself.

What you get

  • 60 active dental organizations in Texas
  • 20 active California organizations found with the NPPES taxonomy search "Psychologist" (psychologists and other mental-health providers; each record shows its registered taxonomy)
  • For each record: NPI, organization name, authorized official (name and title), phone with its source, city, state, ZIP and taxonomy
  • solution.py: a Python 3 script without extra packages that pulls the same kind of list for any US state and taxonomy from the official API

What this data is, and what it is not

  • All records come from the public CMS NPPES NPI Registry, retrieved on 26 October 2026. The registry itself is free; you pay for the cleaned export and the script.
  • The authorized official is the person the organization named in NPPES: often the owner or president, sometimes an office, billing or compliance manager.
  • Phone numbers are as listed in NPPES. Nobody has called or checked them, and many are main office lines rather than direct lines.
  • If you contact these organizations, follow US rules such as the TCPA and the National Do Not Call Registry.
